Become a beta user

Get Investment on your phone before everyone else, and help shape what it becomes. Joining takes about two minutes and is free.

You need: an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) with the Google Play Store, and a Google (Gmail) account. iPhones are not supported.

How to join

Four steps

Use the same Google account for every step: the one signed in to the Play Store on your phone (open Play Store and tap your profile picture to check).

  1. Join the beta users group

    Open the Towfiq's App Beta Users group on Google Groups and tap Join group. No need to send us anything.

    Join the beta users group

  2. Accept the invitation

    On your phone, open the link below. Google Play shows a Become a tester button: tap it to switch on early access.

    Get early access

  3. Install the app

    Install Investment from Google Play. It updates by itself like any other app.

    On a computer? Scan the code with your phone's camera.

  4. Use it for 14 days

    Keep the app installed and stay in the group and the beta for at least 14 days. Open it now and then, add an investment, and tell us what you think.

Help

Something not working?

  • "App not available" or "not found": make sure you joined the group with the same Google account the Play Store uses, then try the links again.
  • Several Google accounts on the phone: switch the Play Store to the account you joined the group with before opening the links.
  • Just joined? It can take a few minutes, sometimes a few hours, before the Play Store lets you in. Try again later.
  • Please don't leave the group during the beta. Leaving removes your access to the app.

Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Email towfiq106@gmail.com, or leave private feedback on the app's Google Play page. Only we can see beta users' feedback.

Your privacy

Group membership is only used to give you access to the beta. The app keeps your records on your phone and, if you turn on backup, in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
