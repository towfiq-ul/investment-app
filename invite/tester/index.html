Investment

Become a Tester

Thanks for helping test Investment, an Android app that keeps track of money invested with partners and splits the returns fairly. Before it goes public on Google Play, it has to be tested by real people for at least 14 days.

You need: an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) with the Google Play Store, and a Google (Gmail) account. Joining takes about two minutes. iPhones are not supported. Testing is free.

How to join

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the link to the Towfiq's App Beta Users group on Google Groups and tap Join group. Use the Google account that is signed in to the Play Store on your phone (open Play Store and tap your profile picture to check). No need to send us anything.

    Join the testers group

  2. Accept the invitation

    On your phone, open the link below while signed in with the same Google account, then tap Become a tester.

    Join the test

  3. Install the app

    Install Investment from Google Play. It updates by itself like any other app.

    Open in Google Play

  4. Use it for 14 days

    Keep the app installed and stay in the group and the test for at least 14 days. Open it now and then, try adding an investment, and tell us what you think.

Something not working?

  • "App not available" or "not found": make sure you joined the group with the same Google account the Play Store uses, then try the links again.
  • Several Google accounts on the phone: switch the Play Store to the account you joined the group with before opening the links.
  • Just joined? It can take a few minutes, sometimes a few hours, before the Play Store lets you in. Try again later.
  • Please don't leave the group during the test. Leaving removes your access to the app.

Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Email towfiq106@gmail.com, or leave private feedback on the app's Google Play page (testers' feedback is only visible to us).

Your privacy

Group membership is only used to give you access to the test. The app keeps your records on your phone and, if you turn on backup, in your own Google Drive. Read the full privacy policy.
