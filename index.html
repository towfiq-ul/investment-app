Why Investment

Partnership money gets messy fast

Partners join at different times, put in different amounts, and returns arrive month after month. A spreadsheet can't keep up for long.

Without the app

  • Formulas break quietly and nobody notices
  • Later partners get a cut of income from before they joined
  • Splits are off by a few paisa and never add up
  • Arguments over "who is owed what"
  • One lost phone or laptop and the records are gone

With Investment

  • Rules are built in and checked; bad entries are rejected
  • Each return is split by the capital held on the day it was received
  • Every split adds up exactly to the amount received
  • Everyone can see their capital, payouts and yield
  • Automatic backup to your own Google Drive

How it works

Up and running in five minutes

No setup, no account to create. Just your Google account.

  1. Sign in with Google

    One tap. If you already have a backup in your Drive, the app offers to restore it.

  2. Add your investments

    Record each contribution: date, partner, amount and which investment (e.g. Bus-0001). Or import your existing Excel file or Google Sheet.

  3. Record returns as they arrive

    Pick the investment, the months it covers and the amount received. The split preview shows each partner's share before you save.

  4. Close an investment when it ends

    Tick "With capital" on the final return. Everyone gets their capital back plus their share of profit, and the investment is locked.

  5. Check the numbers anytime

    Dashboard for the big picture, Summary for every partner and every payout. Tap any entry to edit or delete it safely.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Investment is free to download and use, and it will stay free.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Investment is for Android phones (Android 8.0 or newer).

What happens if I lose my phone?

Install the app on your new phone, sign in with the same Google account and restore your backup from Google Drive in one tap.

Can the developer see my data?

No. The developer runs no servers. Your data is on your phone and in your own Google Drive only.
